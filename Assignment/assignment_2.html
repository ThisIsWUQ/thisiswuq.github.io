Ideas for My Final Project

1. Communual Archives

People can share artefacts they recognise significant to their life and tell stories about them, whether they are film posters from the 80s, floppy disks, toy collecion, bus ticket, etc. At the same time, other users can share opinions or experiences to those artfects, outlining the shape of time together!

References

National Archives Singapore Australian Queer Archives

2. Map of Urban Legends / Folk Tales

In this website, the geographical location where a tale likely originates is displayed, and its connections to legends or tales of similar themes or motifs are shown. The idea is to highlight the universality of stories or narratives across the world, opening oppurtunity to explore or track how tale is dispersed.
